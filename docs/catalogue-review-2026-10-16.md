# Catalogue source review — 16 October 2026

This is a targeted review of five charities, covering 12 roles. It includes the
four charity records with local edits and North London Action for the Homeless,
whose current advert differed from its catalogue role. It is not a full catalogue
sign-off: the other 25 charities with roles still need current source checks
before any claim that all 76 listings are freshly verified.

## Decisions

| Charity | Source evidence | Catalogue decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Depaul UK | [Nightstop volunteer page](https://www.depaul.org.uk/nightstop-volunteer/) offers a host sign-up and says hosts choose evenings on a flexible rota. The [Nightstop FAQ](https://www.depaul.org.uk/nightstop-faqs/) separately describes drivers and chaperones, says a car is not always needed, and invites people to get in touch; it gives no frequency or shift length for that role. | Keep the host's open status and flexible rota. For transport, remove the unsupported two-hour duration and "as and when" schedule, use unknown commitment and recruitment status, and link to the FAQ rather than the host sign-up page. Do not list driving as required for chaperones. |
| Glass Door | The [winter shelter page](https://www.glassdoor.org.uk/winter-night-shelter-volunteers) gives weekly 6–9pm shifts from early November to April, but says the shelters are currently closed. The [interest form](https://www.glassdoor.org.uk/forms/express-your-interest-in-volunteering-with-glass-door) says opportunities for new volunteers are limited. The [casework triage page](https://www.glassdoor.org.uk/casework-triage-volunteering) says applications are currently closed. The [Lived Experience Group page](https://www.glassdoor.org.uk/lived-experience-group) invites interested people to apply and says the group meets five times a year. | Keep winter recruitment unknown, casework closed, and the group open. Keep the weekly winter and casework commitments. Record the group's five meetings a year as stated, but use unknown for the frequency category because the catalogue has no five-times-a-year value. Remove lived experience from skills; it belongs under eligibility. |
| Manna Society | The [volunteer page](https://mannasociety.org.uk/support-us/volunteer-for-us/) confirms the kitchen and IT roles, their tasks, hours and SE1 address. It does not state weekly frequency, a three-month minimum, or whether either role is currently recruiting. It mentions that group volunteering may sometimes be offered, without tying that to either named role. | Keep the stated shifts and unknown recruitment status. Remove the unsupported weekly/three-month claims, and leave role-level frequency and applicant type unknown. Both links return to the charity's volunteer information page, which provides a contact route. |
| Simon Community | The [volunteering page](https://www.simoncommunity.org.uk/volunteering/) states that live-in work is full time with a six-month minimum, recommends a regular weekly or fortnightly commitment for part-time roles without making it a rule, and says extra minibus drivers are needed. The [contact page](https://www.simoncommunity.org.uk/contact/) says volunteer enquiries are high and spaces are limited; it requires volunteers to be UK-based or able to relocate at their own expense. | Remove the unsupported twelve-month maximum and driving preference from the live-in role. Retain the six-month minimum and age 18 requirement, add the relocation condition, and mark live-in and general outreach availability unknown. Describe the part-time cadence as flexible with the weekly/fortnightly recommendation. Keep minibus recruitment open, the over-25 and clean-licence requirement, and remove the unsupported two-hour shift length. Link all three roles to the direct contact form. |
| North London Action for the Homeless | Its [current volunteer page](https://www.nlah.org.uk/volunteer/) says it is recruiting for washing-up shifts on Mondays 1–3pm and Wednesdays 7–9pm, with an application form linked from the page. The linked form is a DOCX that the browser reader could not inspect. The page no longer mentions the Supplies Driver role. | Rename the meal-service notice to Washing-up Volunteer, correct the tasks and shift times, and retain open status. Set frequency to unknown because the page gives days and times but not how often a volunteer is expected. Set the Supplies Driver's recruitment status to unknown because the current page does not mention it. Keep the live charity page as the route until the linked document can be downloaded and checked directly. |
